## Executed, limited design

Six families `M,N,C,S,L,R`, intervention `A` versus sham `A0`, **2 independent societies per family/policy cell**, **4 actors × 4 periods** per society; 24 societies, 96 periods, 384 actor-periods total. Seeds `303000..303023`, fixed in the merged pilot runner **before outputs existed**. Two-atom H4 truth configurations were intentionally selected as engineering smoke cases, **not** an independently declared or representative `G_evaluation`. Society-level `A` assigns random/choice matching with equal probabilities and fines independently by dyad with equal probabilities; `A0` assigns only random matching and zero fines. A and A0 use *different seeds*, **not paired intervention forks**.

Source and analysis read each trial's Hiyal verified world, but **only the files under the original run's `public-only/` export**, containing `period,regime,matching,fine_bits,actions,payoffs`, are eligible to be considered as input for a *future* exploratory classifier. **Neither this `results/` directory nor the aggregate JSON may be loaded as classifier features, targets, or held-out validation.** The run's Hiyal internals, private truth manifest, label mapping, and entire provenance/summary bundle are audit-only and must be kept outside the classifier's ingest path. A pathname label saying `PRIVATE` is not an access control; removing these 24 rows narrows public exposure but does not replace a physically separated ingest boundary.

The run checked all 96 public records for the allowed six-field public schema, lawful regime/fines/matchings/actions, exact dyadic payoff identities, monotone periods, fixed allocation and strict 384 actor-period budget.

Across the **equally weighted chosen pilot cells**: A `53/192 = 27.6042%` cooperation; A0 `28/192 = 14.5833%`; observed difference `+13.0208` percentage points. These 192 individual actor-period observations **are not 192 independent statistical units**.

Physical-payoff sample means were `15.5/192 = 0.08073` under A versus `28/192 = 0.14583` under A0. The **mechanical payoff identity** includes a defector's negative fine term, while cooperation does not itself incur that fine. Thus an arm can record a lower aggregate material-payoff average despite a higher *observed* cooperation count. **This particular A/A0 mean difference cannot be attributed to fines, partner choice, or any causal welfare tradeoff:** there are only two separately seeded societies per family/policy cell, high sampling noise, and A combines two operators. Within M and N, for example, the observed cooperation counts happen to be equal while A payoffs are lower; the penalty accounting is mechanical, but the between-arm pattern is not a general effect estimate.

Under A, the 12 societies had **21 choice-assigned periods of 48** and **58 half-fined dyads of 96**, while all A0 periods and dyads obeyed sham assignment. These are finite random assignment counts, not approximate 50/50 guarantees for every tiny sample.

## Interpretation, limits and next research priorities

**What this supports:** The merged Rust code and pinned Hiyal engine run all six mechanisms in a common physical world, produce replayable event histories, enforce public-schema restrictions, and are deterministic for identical declared seeds. Elementary physical accounting and intervention supports hold on all actual generated pilot records. The deliberately chosen statistics—cooperation counts, payoff sums, choice-regime/fine assignment counts and replay equality—were successfully derived from the generated records, establishing only that the software-level aggregation checks are executable.

**What it does not support:** No empirical human sociological finding; no fitted rival models; no power estimate, confidence-set calibration or 95% coverage; no assertion that 12 random pilot cells demonstrate all six family-law separations; no full 15-pair H4 proof. As already shown in the specification, `M⊆N,C,S,L,R` and `S⊆N⊆L` preserve unavoidable structural non-identification in important parameter regions. The specific C-to-L frozen-partner map fails with rematching, **not** every possible free-H4 rival law. These theoretical facts were not themselves experimentally proved by the pilot.

**Suggested bounded next phase:**

1. Construct a **deterministic/exact tiny-world identification benchmark** with finite action/matching support and documented fixed-vector versus H4 distinctions, initially at T=1–2. Inspect known structural nested aliases before any classifier training.
2. Analyze **C/L rematching** and **M/N fine-response** at fixed public histories, comparing full conditional matching-and-action laws under legal parameter maps and free-rival alternatives. Mark witness-only inequalities and globally unknown free-H4 relationships honestly.
3. If further stochastic exploration is useful, predeclare a *separate*, small, independently seeded factorial decomposition (fine-only vs matching-only) and a separate truth hierarchy. Do not retrofit it into the existing policy A/A0 pilot or use these outputs as held-out confirmation.
4. Continue independent Python/Rust cross-verification, composite-null methods/coverage, open-world adequacy and 15-pair identification as **scientific acceptance gates**, not implementation blockers.

**Disposition:** Good exploratory **engineering** result; R-06 confirmatory P0 and scientific GO remain **OPEN**.
