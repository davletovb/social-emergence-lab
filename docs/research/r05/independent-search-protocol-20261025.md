## Equal bounded discovery for every narrow formulation
Apply independently to **seven formulations A1, A2, B, C, D, E, F** (six topic areas, two A variants). Make **four logged queries per narrow formulation with equal capacity for synthetic/theoretical and empirical studies**, in scholarly indexes, primary journal pages or another declared source. Replace placeholders with the candidate's actual mechanisms and **unaltered physical intervention**:

1. **S1 — structural/theoretical identification:** `<phenomenon> <rival mechanisms> observational equivalence structural identifiability theory`.
2. **S2 — computational/model-recovery predecessor:** `<phenomenon> <rival mechanisms> model recovery simulation generative model comparison`.
3. **E1 — empirically assigned operator:** `<phenomenon> <physical intervention> randomized field laboratory experiment treatment assignment`.
4. **E2 — closest substantive empirical comparator:** `<phenomenon> <rival mechanisms> measurement causal experiment evidence`.

**Fixed slots:** two S and two E, **five** first nonduplicate scholarly results per query (up to **20** total per formulation), with exact query strings, engine, date, search filters/order and actual hit counts recorded. The S slots must actively seek theoretical *nonidentifiability* proofs and failed recoveries, not only successful simulation classification. An abstract-only paper cannot substitute for a theoretical Methods/proof comparison. The E slots must seek actual assignments and observed outcomes. If a search service lacks a class of work, document the shortfall and alternate source **without reallocating unused slots to the favored track**. Apply the identical method and cap to all seven narrow formulations. Examine primary abstract/introduction/methods where accessible. **No source names are supplied** in the independent reader packet.

Then select **the two closest primary predecessors discovered by those queries** using relevance to the narrow intervention (not citation counts). For each, inspect **up to three references it cites and up to three works that cite it**, selected for actual similarity to the intervention/estimand. Total planned bounded expansion per formulation: **up to 12 citation leads**; deduplicate and log inaccessible/reference-tool failures. Record which of the screened leads are a *direct test*, a *related mechanism* or a *conceptual/methodological predecessor*. Do not equate a paper appearing in search results with an inspected full text.

Prioritization: examine at least one **original primary work's substantive Methods, formal-model definitions/proof, or experimental design section** per narrow formulation when accessible (ideally at least one S and one E comparator). For theory-only work, proofs/identification assumptions replace human experiment Methods; otherwise mark this unmet rather than substituting an abstract and declaring the gate passed. Within **both S and E search slots**, include a check for counterexamples, observational equivalence, negative findings or null effects, reporting only what actually surfaced within the capped result set. No recursive unbounded citation exploration. Source volume is an effort limit, not a guarantee of novelty.

## Fair source selection and decision
- **Before seeing source results:** separately record the proposed operator, actual observable unit, the two claim routes S (synthetic exact/model-identification claim) and E (empirical human correspondence), and possible null/overlap. Do **not assign 0–3 scores or advance/defer verdicts** before sources.
- **After search:** record the three strongest original predecessors, observed designs and exactly which candidate intervention/estimand they do and do not already test.
- Screen the **same question under S and E separately** for each candidate; an eligible S can lack human data; E requires participant records, intervention assignment, timing, ethics and rights, not just a public paper or synthetic archive.
- **No predetermined three-candidate shortlist:** an independent reviewer first returns seven (A1/A2 separate) conditional verdicts; reconcile with the initial author's new *same-procedure* assessment later. Document disagreements source-by-source. Candidate variants of A are not assigned a preferred winner before this step.
- Do not turn lack of search hits into a claim of global novelty; **UNKNOWN** is acceptable. Lack of original Methods access remains a marked gate limitation.
- A reviewer can suggest new papers; each must be checked against its actual claim before being used to change a candidate's status.
