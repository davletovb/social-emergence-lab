## 2026-10-25 — A2 six mechanism rules and C institutional legitimacy negative result

- [A2 six-generator candidate formal specification](a2-six-generator-formal-spec-20261025.md): explicit common dyadic game, action/partner ranking and learning kernels for pure material incentive **M**, personal norm **N**, conditional response **C**, fixed-type **S** assortative choice, enforcement-masked social-belief learner **L** and genuinely different aspiration/action-value reinforcement learner **R**. Identifies nested boundary overlaps, admissible observables and **a physical information leak**: exact monetary payoff plus own action/fine may reveal partner action even when history is supposedly hidden. **Candidate equations only**, no validation across the six mechanisms, no pilot or Hiyal implementation.
- [C institution monitoring-cost/legitimacy audit](c-institution-cost-legitimacy-audit-20261025.md) and [four exact fraction reference checks](c-cost-legitimacy-fixture-20261025.csv): verifies Kosfeld et al. (2009) **fixed institution cost at 2 while changing public-good MPCR**, Dickson et al. (2022) **randomized realized enforcement accuracy conditional on authority accuracy investment**, and Anderson/Putterman (2006) **varied peer punishment price** (all different physical operators). Cost-only changes cannot generally identify material expected gain versus legitimacy when `g+v` alone drives adoption. Restricted exact toy demonstrates that failure at all costs. **A new exclusion-valid intervention would be a re-reviewed amendment, not free novelty credit.**

**Implication:** A2 has a written six-family *candidate model* but requires independent rules review, feedback/visibility redesign and actual identifying contrasts. C's **original single monitoring-cost shift fails the general unique-mechanism criterion**; keep as a negative/boundary investigation only, or consider D as a separate Tier-2 alternative after comparative review. Neither source audit warrants a human behavioral claim, numerical rubric, final R-05 decision, R-06 freeze or simulation.
