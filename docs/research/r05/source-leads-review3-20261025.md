Levy's [publisher-supplied replication-package link](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20191777) and the reviewer-named [openICPSR DOI 10.3886/E121922V1](https://doi.org/10.3886/E121922V1) are **leads, not file inventories/confirmed reuse rights**.
