## Common prospective rule (apply to all six)

**No hard rank or numerical rubric scores at Tier 1.** For each narrow variant, (1) inspect its *closest relevant original scientific predecessor* to an explicitly recorded source depth, including any known refutation/duplication of the proposed contrast; (2) articulate a same-world rival contrast **OR an informative structural non-identification limit**, not mere optimizer/sample failure; (3) name a bounded independent mathematical/experimental reference path; (4) evaluate whether that evidence can support the **actual intended claim** (synthetic identification vs correspondence to real humans, and assignment/timing compatibility). A generic existing effect **does not itself** establish that every new identification question is already answered; equally, “no usable data” is not a disqualifier for a bounded *synthetic* identifiability claim.

**Novelty/decisive predecessor condition:** if a prior study directly tests the *same question and contrast*, the candidate must articulate an as-yet-unresolved alternative or be **deferred**. An abstract suggesting overlap prompts **a source-specific methods check**, not automatic rejection. Missing original-source access is **UNKNOWN/PENDING**, never scored as failure or zero.

**Two depth levels:** basic verified original publication sections for all six; closer original methods/supplements and accessible data metadata for each contender, **to comparable scrutiny before any decision to advance/defer**. A secondary **dossier-only** reader must rescreen a [status-stripped source packet](blinded-screening-packet.md) **without** seeing the past shortlist or #36 thread. The earlier shortlist was committed alongside its rule, so it was **not a genuinely preregistered rule**. This revision is prospectively applied to the next comparison, not misrepresented as a preexisting freeze.

## Third review — screening procedure amended prospectively, 2026-10-25

Following [Issue #37's third review](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/37#issuecomment-6388431046), this screen remains **retracted/UNRESOLVED**. Previous “equal depth” only meant citations named by prior reviewers; it did not apply a controlled independent source-discovery method. The [future independent source-search procedure](independent-search-protocol-20261025.md) therefore defines **four balanced original search queries per narrow candidate (two synthetic identification/model-recovery and two empirical intervention/comparator slots)**, equal result/abstract/methods limits and bounded backward/forward citation chasing. This procedure did **not** generate the initial shortlists or earlier corrections and is **not claimed to be preregistered for those outcomes**.

A [new neutral second-reader packet](blinded-screening-packet.md) contains only research questions, candidate mechanisms and review criteria, **no prior sources or verdicts**. To prevent repository/history leakage, provide it as **a standalone attachment** and register reviewer model/session, tools, received materials and actual access paths. Do not ask the previous Claude reviewer or the assistant already exposed to this thread to certify itself as an independent screener. The second reader must discover its own predecessors first and return an unedited date-stamped verdict **before** reading our [separate source-lead notes](source-leads-review3-20261025.md).

**Claim-priority sensitivity precommitted for the new review:** Every candidate is screened as two different proposals where coherent: **S** synthetic model identification (a rigorous exact/non-identification result may be valuable without human data), and **E** empirical human correspondence (requires actual human data design, observed fields, assignment/timing, independent units, permissions). Compare these routes **before the owner chooses which research contribution to prioritize**, rather than choosing retrospectively based on the shortlist. No owner preference or approval is invented.

**Additional direct comparators newly identified**: social influence vs quality (Salganik et al. 2006), social-norm expression/sanction information (Bursztyn, Egorov & Fiorin 2020) and directly differentiated pluralistic ignorance vs coordination (Smerdon et al. 2020), randomized referral incentives (Beaman & Magruder 2012) and three referral field studies (Pallais & Sands 2016), punishment price (Anderson & Putterman 2006), and heterogeneous randomized social-media contact responses (Levy 2021; Nyhan et al. 2023). [Details and verification limits](source-leads-review3-20261025.md). These are not assumed to settle all candidate-specific structural-identification questions; they increase the burden for novelty. A separate scarcity shock/governance predecessor (Amirova & Petrick 2022) is also recorded. Source-verified claims are narrow; participant data have not been inspected.
