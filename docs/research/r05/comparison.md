## Method and guards

- **Scope control:** rank the *original physical intervention* from each dossier on claim-specific evidence; new randomized rights, matching signals, graph rewires, identity primes and candidate-pool randomization proposed by a reader are **design amendments**, not free novelty/identification points. R-05 selection cannot rely on a newly added arm unless all competing candidates get an equally re-reviewed amended dossier.
- **S and E separately:** S = restricted synthetic identification/theory including valuable non-identifiability; E = *genuinely feasible* human empirical correspondence (existing data with suitable actual assignment, or concrete ethically recruitable/costed experiment). A theoretical possible future human trial is **not** an audited E pass.
- **No simple weighted sum or artificial score precision:** existing framework's **six 0–3 dimensions** will be formally fixed after remaining original Methods/variable-rights checks. The qualitative matrices here are evidence triage, with **U unknown** where necessary. An experiment is selected by an explicitly recorded owner decision **after** that full evidence check; none ready remains allowed.
- **Novelty = narrow question, not mechanism words:** full A1 longitudinal twin non-identification and basic early success already published; B pluralistic-ignorance preference-disclosure effects already published; E forced/voluntary exposure and identity salience studied; C legitimacy-fairness channel already experimentally identified. A2's six-way *joint* recovery and C's monitoring-cost/selection question remain *candidate untested combinations*, **not verified gaps**.

Independent source-level verifier and a full six-family recoverability study are still open.
