**Scientifically important nuance:** the mathematical statement “different parameterized models with the same full observed action kernel are observationally equivalent” is true by definition; a contribution would need a nontrivial mechanism pair, observation/assignment constraints and proof or falsification that practical interventions cannot break that equivalence. Likewise, B's report writes `R` as “removal of audience/sanction exposure”; the actual issue/dossier specifies **sanction removal**, distinct from anonymous-audience removal. Before protocol freezing, keep `R` physically defined as **the actual sanction-removal operator** (with announcement/observability specified). Anonymity is a separate intervention that could affect multiple channels.

## Selective independent source checks by report auditor (2026-10-25)

- **Srivastava, `Familiar Strangers`**: the [author's 2025 job-market paper](https://kartiksrivastava94.github.io/pages/Srivastava_JobReferrals.pdf) specifically experiments with allocating referral invitations to lower-caste incumbents; the [2026 Harvard dissertation](https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/ad06fee1-0500-44d8-913a-4c5e7882b29b) corroborates the design at the abstract level. This makes a generic randomized referral-access novelty claim weak, but is **not** direct evidence that all employer-screening estimands have been addressed.
- **Kosfeld, Okada & Riedl**: [*Institution Formation in Public Goods Games*](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.99.4.1335) was published **2009** in *American Economic Review* (the report encountered a 2006 discussion paper), with replication/supplementary materials listed. Its original article establishes endogenous institution formation, not the specific institution-wide monitoring-cost manipulation proposed here.
- **Safarzynska**: [*The Impact of Resource Uncertainty and Intergroup Conflict on Harvesting*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10640-017-0193-9) was published online in **2017**, in a **2018 journal issue**, and experiments with resource shocks and conflict in a renewable commons. The exact four-way rival-generator comparison remains to be substantiated.

These three spot checks support parts of the report's *related-work identification*. They **do not validate** all primary papers, exact assignment details, conclusions or each seven-category verdict. No raw participant data were accessed in these checks.
