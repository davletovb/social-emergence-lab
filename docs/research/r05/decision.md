## Research contribution and explicitly chosen claim level

**Primary contribution chosen by this owner decision:** **synthetic mechanism identification (S)**—learn when distinct fully specified action/matching/learning mechanisms *can or cannot* be distinguished by feasible observables and common physical interventions. This is **not an empirical correspondence (E) validation of human cooperation**. The prospective lab-style *one realized randomized history per independent unit* can be used as an observation/assignment constraint in synthetic research; no participant recruitment is authorized. A historical Galbiati-like arm is separately limited to actual source-recorded assignments; human-data rights and variable coverage are not yet established.

**Narrow question:** Under the same repeated dyadic cooperation/payoff world and an identical bounded observation and assignment budget, can the six named generator families be *uniquely identified* from public action, fine, partner-matching and time records? At which nested, near-overlap or intervention-support settings is the identified set multi-family, even when forecasting remains accurate?

**Six candidates:** material enforcement **M**, personal normative preference **N**, conditional response **C**, fixed-type assortative partner selection **S**, fine-masked conditional belief learning **L**, aspiration/action-value reinforcement learning **R**. This is a restricted predeclared synthetic generator set, not a discovery of “six true types of people.”
