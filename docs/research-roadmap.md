# Research roadmap

**Updated: 2026-10-22.** Experiment 001 and Experiment 002 V0–V3 are **exploratory synthetic-model research**. No stage is externally preregistered, empirically calibrated to a human population or confirmed as a novel sociological law.

## Mission

Investigate macro-level social patterns emerging from individual interactions using explicit mechanisms, falsifiable hypotheses and reproducible computational experiments. Hiyal is the simulation engine; this repository owns mechanisms, study protocols, analysis and interpretations.

## Research sequence

1. **001 — Emergence of inequality:** exploratory known Pólya-urn benchmark completed; [confirmatory protocol draft](../experiments/001-emergence-of-inequality/confirmatory-protocol-draft.md) is not registered.
2. **002 — Posttruth opportunity recovery / structural memory:** 36-parent V2 decomposition and **432-parent, nine-condition V3 factorial completed**. [V3 Stage A](../experiments/002-reputation-lock-in/results/v3-stage-a-20261022/README.md) and [Stage B](../experiments/002-reputation-lock-in/results/v3-stage-b-20261022/README.md) found **no cell satisfying positive active-regeneration criteria**; fast belief-dependent deletion showed compensatory new-link exposure. The models favor slow relaxation of inherited opportunities at studied parameters. Stage A's frozen criterion was **not fully met**, and Stage B's rule was amended *after* Stage A outcomes: neither stage is confirmatory. See [full protocol](../experiments/002-reputation-lock-in/v3-protocol-20261022.md), [independent-review packet](experiment-002-v3-independent-review-packet.md), and [Issue #19](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/19).
3. **003 — Emergence of cooperation:** possible future work on reciprocity, enforcement and composition; no implementation plan approved.
4. **004 — Social norms and institutional persistence:** possible future work on convention stabilization and replacement.
5. **005 — Polarization and information networks:** possible future work on network topology and selective exposure.

## Immediate gate — scientific review and provenance, not another simulation

- **Scientific integrity:** secure an external **human** theory/methods review and a variable-level, rights-verified independent empirical benchmark audit; [Issue #22](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/22) and [review packet](experiment-002-v3-independent-review-packet.md). Do not equate an automated desk review or a mathematical exposure identity with original scientific evidence.
- **V3 raw evidence:** 432-parent stage results have committed SHA manifests and summaries, but approximately **9.5 GB of full Hiyal journals were not durably archived**. [Issue #21](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/21) specifies provenance-preserving regeneration / custody requirements.
- **Existing expiring evidence:** original V0 Actions ZIP expires **2026-11-04** [#12](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/12), ahead of V1/V2 and Experiment 001 January 2027 deadlines ([#14](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/14), [#17](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/17), [#6](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/6)). Locally checked copies do not fulfill the user-controlled durable archive/retrieval gate.

**Decision:** pause further expensive factorial/parameter sweeps. The next scientifically meaningful progress comes from independent challenge of the structural-memory account, honest negative/compensation findings, and suitable external measurements—not larger volumes of synthetic trials.

## Stage gates

- G0: theory, estimands, interventions, parameters and decision rule declared before results, with dated amendments preserved.
- G1: algebraic expectation, symmetry, boundary, replay, restart and ancestry checks pass.
- G2: exploratory sample reports uncertainty across **parent societies**, not between dependent forks/events.
- G3: protocol/code/seed domains preserved; never relabel retrospective or post hoc comparisons as preregistered.
- G4: all outcomes, nulls, failures, per-cell controls and original or explicitly regenerated raw evidence are independently auditable.
- G5: external human scientific critique and lawful variable-level empirical calibration established **before** generalizing to humans or proposing strong publication claims.

## Research integrity

Keep experiment development, exploratory analyses, post hoc amendments and any future confirmatory studies distinct. Preserve complete raw evidence with independently recorded hashes and durable user-controlled retention; regenerating matching bytes is useful but not the same as custody of originals. Report null/negative findings, not only apparent positive effects. Never claim human causation or universal social laws from a synthetic agent model.
