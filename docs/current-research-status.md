# Research and implementation status — 28 October 2026

**Scope:** as-of snapshot of what is **implemented**, **executed**, **analytically proposed**, and **not yet scientifically accepted** in Social Emergence Lab. This document reconciles the current repository at commit [`2cc0c225`](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/commit/2cc0c225b5b0954cd6c79f127c2012431b6d4c5c). It does **not** rewrite the historical meaning or registration timing of earlier protocols and run reports.

**Lab mission:** test how explicit agent interactions generate social patterns, determine when different generative explanations are observationally distinguishable, and build reproducible methodological tools. The generic event-sourced Rust engine is a separate project, [Hiyal](https://github.com/davletovb/hiyal); this repository implements scientific models and owns protocols, analysis and custody records. The current Cargo manifest pins Hiyal to `050d1de094f5d0813817e2020ec5c5da97d5c42e` (Rust **1.89**, edition 2024).

## Experiment 002 — the negative finding matters

[V2 decomposition and reanalysis](../experiments/002-reputation-lock-in/results/v2-36-paired-20261021/README.md) showed a late access gap of **+0.2183** but its **new-link plus belief-mediated selection** component averaged only **+0.0016** (exploratory interval **−0.0214 to +0.0246**). [V3 Stage A](../experiments/002-reputation-lock-in/results/v3-stage-a-20261022/README.md) and [Stage B](../experiments/002-reputation-lock-in/results/v3-stage-b-20261022/README.md) extended this with a 432-parent, nine-condition investigation. **No cell met its positive active-regeneration criterion.** Stage A's rule was not fully met; Stage B's criterion was amended *after* Stage A. The defensible interpretation is **slow relaxation of an inherited network**, not proof of an independently active post-correction mechanism.

**Evidence custody:** V0 and V3 original raw artifacts were **replay-verified and published as [GitHub Releases](experiment-002-evidence-custody-20261022.md)**; V3 owner copy was additionally hash-matched. V1/V2 permanent independent custody remains open ([#14](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/14), [#17](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/17)). GitHub Releases are not DOI-backed archival repositories. External methods/novelty review remains deferred ([#22](https://github.com/davletovb/social-emergence-lab/issues/22)).

## Experiment 003 — what now exists

**Selected on 2026-10-25**, with R-05 screening exceptions recorded in [the signed decision](research/r05/decision.md). **Current candidate normative model:** [v0.2.2 full J/H4 law](research/r06/a2-v022-joint-law-hierarchy-20261025.md) **plus inherited [v0.2.1 six action/update mechanisms](research/r06/a2-v021-six-kernel-design-20261025.md)**. Versions v0.1, v0.2 and v0.2.1 remain dated historical drafts; no confirmatory protocol has been frozen.

The six named synthetic families are **M** material fines, **N** stable norm/preference, **C** conditional cooperation, **S** type-linked sorting, **L** social-belief learning and **R** contextual reinforcement. A and A0 share the same four-actor dyadic game; A randomizes matching regime and dyad fine, whereas A0 always uses random matching and no fine. The **J law** uses independent simultaneous actor innovations *conditional on the latent state and current assignments*. In **H4**, each actor independently draws a **joint parameter tuple** from the same unknown ≤4-atom measure G (within-tuple correlations allowed); one deterministic G and common scalar parameters apply across independent societies. Do **not** treat actors within a society or their time steps as independent empirical replicates.

**Implemented and executed (engineering / exploratory only):**

- [`src/experiment003.rs`](../src/experiment003.rs) implements all six mechanisms, shared broker and fine/payoff rules, H4 validation, state updates and public-only records. [`src/bin/exp003-pilot.rs`](../src/bin/exp003-pilot.rs) is a manually invoked runner with Hiyal journal/setup/replay integration.
- The [first bounded pilot](../experiments/003-cooperation-mechanism-recovery/results/exploratory-pilot-20261026.md) generated and replay-verified **24 independent synthetic societies**, **4 actors × 4 periods**, **384 actor-periods**, two societies per family/policy cell. The two policies have **different seeds** and are not matched counterfactual forks. Truth-labelled evidence and internal journal data are **audit-only**, not inputs to a mechanism classifier.
- [The exact-support benchmark](../experiments/003-cooperation-mechanism-recovery/results/exact-support-benchmark-20261026.md) enumerates **384 categorical outcomes** for one A period (48 for A0) and selected conditional continuations; it is **f64 categorical enumeration**, not symbolic exact real arithmetic or global parameter optimization.
- [Restricted free-rival search v0.2](../experiments/003-cooperation-mechanism-recovery/results/restricted-h4-free-rival-v02-20261027.md) gives a certified *analytic event* lower bound for a **specific N truth versus any M H4 representation** under A, plus limited C/L searches. The numerical best-fit search **does not certify** a continuous/global C/L minimum or a confidence interval.
- M embeds in all families; **S⊆N⊆L** under the full legal H4 parameter mapping. The proposed [random-rematching](../experiments/003-cooperation-mechanism-recovery/cl-random-broker-certificate-20261027.md), [reverse-C](../experiments/003-cooperation-mechanism-recovery/reverse-C-H4-witnesses-20261027.md) and [remaining-pair invariants](../experiments/003-cooperation-mechanism-recovery/remaining-h4-pair-invariants-20261027.md) give explicit **author-derived synthetic population-law noninclusion witnesses**. Their run reports and independent-formula checks are committed. They are **not** accepted third-party proofs or a calibrated statistical classifier. The corrected N/S **cross-actor Hankel proposal is invalid under actor-iid H4**; N⊄S remains **UNKNOWN**. R⊄L under A0 also remains **UNKNOWN**.
